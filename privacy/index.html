Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 · Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Its The Herringtons LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), a California limited liability company, collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Davey – Personal Finance mobile application and related services (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which may be an Apple private relay address).
  • Budgeting information. Budgets, categories, savings goals, notes, manually entered accounts and transactions, and other content you create in the App.
  • Support communications. Information you send us when you contact support.

Information from connected financial accounts

If you choose to connect a bank or financial account, we use Plaid Inc. (“Plaid”) to gather information from your financial institution. Depending on the account, this may include:

  • Account names, types, and the last four digits of account numbers
  • Current and available balances
  • Transaction history, including amounts, dates, merchant names, and categories
  • Financial institution name

We do not receive or store your bank login credentials. You provide those directly to Plaid. By connecting an account, you acknowledge that Plaid’s collection, use, and handling of your information is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information. Device type, operating system version, app version, and crash or diagnostic data, which may be provided through Apple if you have opted in to share it.
  • Security data. Authentication events and server logs used to detect and prevent unauthorized access.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or health data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App’s budgeting, tracking, and reporting features
  • Display your balances, transactions, budgets, and charts
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Process and manage subscriptions
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service notices
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, security incidents, and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use your financial data to target advertising.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only as described below:

  • Plaid – to connect and retrieve data from your financial institutions at your direction.
  • Supabase – our cloud backend and database provider, which hosts account data and encrypted bank connection tokens.
  • Apple – for Sign in with Apple, iCloud sync of App data stored in your private iCloud account, and App Store subscription billing.
  • Legal and safety – when required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us.
  • Business transfers – in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.
  • With your consent – for any other purpose you authorize.

Our service providers are permitted to use your information only to perform services on our behalf.

4. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS
  • Encryption of bank connection (Plaid access) tokens at rest using AES-256, stored only on our servers and never on your device
  • Database access controls so each user can access only their own data
  • Optional Face ID / passcode lock within the App
  • Multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days, and we revoke your bank connections through Plaid, unless we must retain certain information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Disconnect accounts. You can disconnect any linked financial account at any time in the App’s Settings.
  • Delete your account. You can delete your account and associated data directly in the App’s Settings, or by contacting us.
  • Access and correction. You can view and edit most of your information within the App.
  • Apple settings. You can manage Sign in with Apple and iCloud settings through your Apple ID.

California residents

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), gives you the right to: know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; request deletion of your personal information; correct inaccurate personal information; and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise your rights, contact us using the information below. We will verify your request before responding.

Other U.S. state residents

Residents of other states with consumer privacy laws may have similar rights. Contact us to make a request, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Availability

The App is intended for users in the United States. It is not offered in the European Union, and we do not target users located there.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App or by other appropriate means before the changes take effect. The “Last Updated” date above shows when this policy was last revised.
