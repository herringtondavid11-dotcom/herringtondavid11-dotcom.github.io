Common questions

How do I connect my bank?

Open Davey, tap the gear (Settings), then Banks › Connect a Bank. Davey uses Plaid to connect; your bank password goes to your bank through Plaid, never to Davey.

My bank needs me to sign in again.

Banks sometimes ask you to re-confirm access. Go to Settings › Banks and tap Reconnect next to the bank, or open the account and tap Reconnect.

After signing in to my bank, I ended up in my browser.

Tap Return to Davey on that page. Davey picks up where you left off and finishes connecting.

What is a View?

A View is a group of accounts, such as Personal, Business or Rentals. Pick one at the top of any screen and every number in Davey follows it.

How do I cancel Davey Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Davey. You can also tap Manage Subscription in Davey's Settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

In Davey, open Settings › Delete Account. This disconnects your banks, deletes the data on our server, and erases Davey's data on your devices and in your iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.
